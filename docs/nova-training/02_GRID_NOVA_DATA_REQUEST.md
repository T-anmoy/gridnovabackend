# Data Grid Nova must provide for Nova

Send this list to Grid Nova. Every answer becomes a verified knowledge entry, so each answer should be exact and approved by a named person. Nova will never guess any of these; until answered, it tells the customer a team member will follow up.

**How to answer:** one short paragraph per item, in English (Highline prepares Hindi and Bengali; a native speaker at Grid Nova reviews them). Mark anything that is confidential or must not be said to customers.

## A. Questions customers ask most (highest priority)

| # | Data needed | Why Nova needs it |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | The 30–50 questions customers actually ask sales and associates (from calls, WhatsApp, site visits), with Grid Nova's approved answers | The single most valuable input: real questions, real answers |
| R2 | Common objections and the approved response (e.g. "too expensive", "my roof is weak", "what if I move house", "monsoon/cyclone damage") | Nova handles doubts without inventing claims |
| R3 | The customer journey in Grid Nova's own words: steps from enquiry to handover, and who the customer deals with at each step | Confirms the `customer-journey` entry |
| R4 | Typical timelines: survey after enquiry, proposal after survey, installation duration, net-metering and subsidy approval times (by DISCOM if they differ) | Customers always ask "how long" |
| R5 | Site survey: is it free? who visits? what the customer must prepare | `site-survey` entry |
| R6 | Payment options: bank transfer, cheque, UPI, financing or EMI partners, bank loans for rooftop solar | `unknown-finance` entry |
| R7 | Document checklist for PM Surya Ghar and net metering (electricity bill, ID, bank details, ownership proof, photos…) | `unknown-documents` entry |
| R8 | Cancellation and refund policy after each payment milestone | `unknown-cancellation` entry |
| R9 | Phase and sanctioned-load rules: when a load increase is needed, and whether Grid Nova handles it, per DISCOM (WBSEDCL, CESC, NBPDCL, SBPDCL) | `phase-load` entry |
| R10 | Net metering vs net billing vs gross metering in West Bengal and Bihar, and what a customer gets for surplus units | `net-metering` entry and calculator savings |
| R11 | Service area: all of India, or named states and districts where Grid Nova installs directly | Resolves conflict C2 |
| R12 | After-sales: service phone and email, response time for faults (SLA), what O&M covers after the first year | `service-request`, `om` entries; registry HW-OM |
| R13 | Component warranties: panels (product and performance), inverter, structure, workmanship | Nova currently may only state the system warranty |
| R14 | Which panel makes/models are DCR compliant | Nova must never claim DCR until confirmed |
| R15 | Battery, hybrid and off-grid: offered now or not? | `services-not-yet` entry |
| R16 | Cleaning and maintenance advice customers can follow themselves | Useful, safe content |
| R17 | Insurance for the system (included? optional?) | Common question |
