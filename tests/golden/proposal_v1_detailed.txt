Executive Summary
Grid Nova Energy proposes a 52 kWp grid-connected rooftop solar PV power plant for Example Metal Works at its Amta, Howrah site. The plant is designed to significantly reduce electricity costs, stabilise long-term energy expenses and lower the carbon footprint of the premises. With net metering, surplus solar energy is exported to the grid and adjusted against the energy drawn from the grid.

Solar PV Modules
High-efficiency Mono PERC half-cut solar cells.
High-transparency, low-iron tempered glass for better light transmission, stiffness and impact resistance.
Integrated bypass diodes to protect the cell circuit from hot spots during partial shading.
Strong low-light performance; certified to IEC 61215 and IEC 61730.
String Inverter
Three-phase grid-tied string inverters with multiple MPPT inputs convert the DC power from the solar array into grid-synchronised AC power with precise voltage and stable frequency. The inverters include protection against overload, short circuit and islanding, and support remote monitoring of plant generation through web and mobile platforms.
Module Mounting Structure
Correct mounting maximises power production and protects the investment from wind loads. The mounting structure provides stability and the proper orientation and tilt for the solar array, and will be finalised during detailed engineering based on the roof type and site conditions.
Cables
All cables are selected to minimise losses and to perform reliably at high temperatures. Solar-grade DC cables are UV-resistant and weatherproof for outdoor runs; AC cables are sized for the inverter output and the interconnection point. Final cable sizes and routing will be confirmed during detailed engineering.
Earthing & Lightning Protection
The module mounting structure and all metallic parts of the plant will be properly earthed using an adequate number of earthing kits, to ensure the safety of the power plant. The system is protected by surge protection devices in the DCDB and ACDB, along with a lightning arrester.

CUSTOMER SCOPE OF WORK
Client-side provisions required for smooth installation and commissioning.
Provide approx. 4,300 sq. ft. of shadow-free roof area, clear of obstructions such as trees, water tanks, equipment or dummy pillars.
Provide suitable space for installation of the inverters and electrical panels.
Provide a safe and secure storage area for project materials on receipt at site. After delivery, the customer shall be responsible for the safety and security of the material until it is taken over by Grid Nova Energy’s team for installation.
Provide safe and permanent access to the roof for installation and future maintenance.
Provide a spare feeder / connection point in the existing LT panel or ACDB for interconnection of the solar system.
Provide internet connectivity to the inverter for remote monitoring of energy generation.
Provide power and water required during installation and commissioning.
Provide all applications and documents required for net metering and statutory approvals.
Transformer or contracted-load enhancement, if required as per net-metering regulations, shall be at the customer’s cost.
Any structural modification or civil work, if required, shall be in the customer’s scope.
Statutory inspection (CEIG / DISCOM) fees, if applicable, shall be paid by the customer at actuals.
Storage Area & Inverter Location
The customer is required to provide safe storage space for the material on receipt at site. Inverters are generally IP65 rated and can be installed outdoors; the inverter location will be decided after detailed engineering and site study, preferably on the terrace with adequate space, access and ventilation.
Electrical Works
Cabling between the solar panels, DCDB and inverters will be mostly outdoor and done using UV-resistant solar-grade cables. AC cabling from the inverter to the ACDB and the interconnection point will be done with suitable copper / aluminium cables, routed through existing cable trays or conduits or securely anchored to walls. The exact cable routing will be finalised during detailed engineering.
